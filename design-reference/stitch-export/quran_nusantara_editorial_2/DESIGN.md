## Brand & Style

This design system is crafted for a reverent, distraction-free Quran reading and contemplation platform tailored for Indonesian readers. It embodies a restrained editorial philosophy: the interface exists solely to honor, frame, and clarify sacred text and scholarly Indonesian translation without visual noise or modern consumerist gamification.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Minimalist Editorial & Contemplative:** Prioritizes quiet dignity, typographical balance, and ample breathing space reminiscent of fine printed classical editions (Mushaf) combined with modern high-craft typography.
- **Content-First Dignity:** Complete rejection of aggressive gamification, streak trackers, loud notification badges, decorative banners, and marketing banners. Every layout decision respects the sacred nature of reading and reflection.
- **Nusantara Cultural Resonance:** A refined palette of heritage emerald green, sun-dried warm parchment, and deep charcoal green-black ink reflects the living Islamic scholarly tradition of the Indonesian archipelago.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, physical manuscript sensation. Light mode serves as the default, engineered to reduce optical fatigue during extended study and recitation.

### Palette Roles & Usage
- **Primary (`#145A43`):** Deep regal emerald. Used with discipline for active states, key interactive indicators, ayah highlight emblems, and the subtle "Gus AI" assistant trigger. Never deployed as large solid background blocks.
- **Secondary (`#E8EFEA`):** Pale emerald mist. Reserved for subtle ayah badge containers, active verse indicator bands, and softly tinted contextual callouts.
- **Tertiary Surface (`#EEEEE6`):** Soft muted warm stone. Applied to secondary controls, toolbars, and active tab indicator pills.
- **Canvas / Background (`#F7F5EF`):** Warm parchment tint. The global canvas tone, eliminating harsh blue-white glare while preserving natural contrast.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` / `#FBF9F4`):** Pure white or tinted pearl for floating search sheets, reading setting overlays, and contextual note panels.
- **Text & Headings (`#252923`):** Deep charcoal green-black. Emulates carbon ink pressed onto handmade paper.
- **Muted Metadata (`#666D65`):** Reserved for surah metadata, juz markers, transliterations, and verse indices.
- **Borders & Hairline Rules (`#E2E1D8`):** Restrained dividers for verse demarcation and editorial row separation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates seamless movement between Arabic script, literary Indonesian translation, and precise application chrome.

### Quranic Arabic Script
- **Font Stack:** Prioritize `Amiri`, followed by `Scheherazade New`, and standard Quranic Naskh serifs.
- **Line Heights & Text Flow:** Set Arabic line-height strictly between `2.3` and `2.5` to prevent diacritical marks (tashkeel, tanwin, and tajweed signs) from colliding. Direction is strictly `rtl`.

### Latin Editorial & Interface
- **Headings & Literary Tone:** `Newsreader` delivers an authoritative, scholarly cadence for surah introductions, bismillah ornaments, and longform tafsir annotations.
- **Interface & Translations:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides crisp, neutral legibility for Indonesian translations, index lists, metadata chips, and settings controls. Optimal translation column measure is constrained between `60ch` and `72ch`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored around a centered, single-column reading sanctum, eschewing dense multi-column sidebars during active reading sessions.

### Layout Model
- **Reading Sanctum:** A maximum width of `48rem` (`max-w-3xl`) centered horizontally. This prevents line lengths from exceeding comfortable human reading parameters and keeps eye fatigue low.
- **Index & Discovery Mode:** Surah and Juz navigation displays as structured, single-column editorial lists rather than fragmented card grids.
- **Top Utility Header:** A persistent, low-profile utility bar (`h-14` or `h-16`) containing the brand wordmark, quick surah selector, bookmarks drawer toggle, and contextual reader settings.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column layout with `1rem` margins. The verse view stacks the Arabic text directly above the Indonesian translation with a minimal `space-md` gap.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** Margins expand to `1.5rem`. Reading column stays centered with comfortable side breathing room.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Max container width locked to `48rem` for reading, expanding to `64rem` for side-by-side tafsir drawer panels when explicitly summoned by the reader.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **flat architectural surfaces and subtle hairline borders** rather than physical drop shadows or layered glass effects.

### Principles
- **No Heavy Shadows:** Traditional blurred box-shadows are avoided. All hierarchy is communicated through subtle contrast between `#F7F5EF` (page) and `#FFFFFF` (panels), bounded by `#E2E1D8` hairline strokes.
- **Hairline Demarcation:** 1px borders using `#E2E1D8` separate surah list rows, translation sections, and the fixed top header.
- **Active Ayah Highlighting:** Visual emphasis is achieved through a soft background wash of `#E8EFEA` or a 2px left-accent border in `#145A43`, never through raising or floating the verse card.
- **Modals & Drawers:** When contextual overlays (e.g., search modal, Gus AI prompt, tafsir sheet) appear, they rely on a soft paper surface `#FFFFFF` with a translucent dimming scrim (`rgba(37, 41, 35, 0.25)`) and an ultra-subtle ambient blur (`blur(4px)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **soft, understated geometry** (Level 1). Subtle radiuses soften corners without feeling bubbly, playful, or informal.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Buttons & Interactive Badges:** `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.375rem` (6px) roundedness for functional buttons, input fields, and tab groups.
- **Ayah Number Emblems & Chips:** Subtle pill-shaped or soft rounded boxes (`rounded-md`, 6px) that anchor the verse count neatly next to the Arabic calligraphy.
- **Dialogs & Sheet Drawers:** `0.5rem` (8px) along top edges for mobile bottom-sheets and desktop modal panels, evoking cleanly trimmed book covers.

## Components

### 1. Header & Navigation Bar
- **Structure:** Flat, quiet bar anchored to the top with a 1px bottom border (`#E2E1D8`) on `#F7F5EF` or `#FFFFFF`.
- **QuranNusantara Wordmark:** Rendered in `Newsreader` medium weight, paired with an understated small serif or sans edition signifier.
- **Action Controls:** Icon-only or compact text buttons with hover/tap states transitioning to `#EEEEE6`. Includes search trigger (`⌘K`), quick surah jump, bookmarks counter, and font settings toggle (`Aa`).
- **Gus AI Trigger:** Rendered as a quiet emerald badge button (`#E8EFEA` background, `#145A43` text, hairline border) indicating a contemplative theological assistant, devoid of flashing or neon AI tropes.

### 2. Verse Reading Row (Ayah Unit)
- **Container:** Borderless continuous stream with subtle hairline divider (`#E2E1D8`) between verses, or generous `space-xl` margin-bottom.
- **Ayah Marker:** A compact pill in `#E8EFEA` with `#145A43` numeric text displaying the verse index.
- **Arabic Segment:** Aligned right (`rtl`), styled in `Amiri` at line-height `2.4`, colored `#252923`.
- **Translation Segment:** Aligned left (`ltr`), set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Newsreader` with line-height `1.75` and max-width `68ch`.
- **Inline Utilities:** Discreet secondary actions (Audio play, Copy, Bookmark, Share, Tafsir) appear on hover or subtle verse selection, styled in muted `#666D65`.

### 3. Surah & Navigation List Rows
- **List Architecture:** Full-width rows separated by 1px hairline rules (`#E2E1D8`). Avoid chunky, drop-shadowed cards.
- **Row Content:** Arabic calligraphy title on the right, Indonesian translated title and numbering on the left, with revelation place (Makkiyah/Madaniyah) and ayah counts styled in small muted text (`#666D65`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover and press trigger a gentle shift to `#F1EFE8` without layout expansion or scale transforms.

### 4. Search & Jump Sheet
- **Presentation:** Centered modal dialog or slide-down header sheet on `#FFFFFF` surface with hairline boundary.
- **Direct Search Input:** Clean, unbordered input with `#252923` caret, accompanied by instant filters for Surah name, Ayah number, Juz, or Indonesian translation keywords.

### 5. Translation & Audio Settings Drawer
- **Control Layout:** Segmented tabs for Arabic font scaling (`Small`, `Normal`, `Large`, `Khatib`), translation engine selection (Kemenag RI, Jalalayn, Quraish Shihab), and reciter audio speed.
- **Selectors:** Soft radio pills with `#EEEEE6` idle backgrounds and `#145A43` active fills with `#FFFFFF` text.
